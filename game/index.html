HOW TO PLAY

Move: WASD on foot, WASD to drive. Drag the mouse to look, wheel to zoom, C for camera.
Cars: walk up to any parked or slow car and press E to take it. Police cars are faster — steal one if you dare.
Wanted: stealing, shooting and hurting people raises your wanted level. Police chase harder as stars rise. Hide out of sight and the heat cools off.
Missions: follow the yellow marker. Talk to Mara, finish the job, get paid. Taxi fares appear as green markers.
Combat: click to shoot. Your pistol is always with you; grab weapon and health pickups around the city.
Save: the game autosaves after every mission. Esc, then Save Game, to save any time.
